The short version

  • Your books are yours. We do not sell them, and we do not use them to advertise to you.
  • We collect what the software needs to work, plus what the law requires us to keep.
  • Advertising cookies are off until you turn them on, and you can turn them off again on this page.
  • You can ask for a copy of your data, or for it to be corrected or deleted.

4. Data about other people that you put into Profitna

This is the part most privacy policies skip, and for an accounting product it is the important one.

When you record a customer, a supplier, a staff member or a donor, you are putting their personal data into our software. For that data you are the data controller and we are your data processor. In plain terms:

  • It stays yours. We process it to run the service for you and for nothing else.
  • We do not use it to advertise, to train any model, or to build a profile of anyone.
  • We do not look at it except where we must to fix a fault, answer your support request, or comply with the law — and those occasions are logged.
  • If one of your customers asks you to correct or delete their data, that is your request to answer. We will help you do it.
  • Your own obligations still apply. You need a lawful basis for the data you enter about other people, and you must tell them how you use it. Profitna being compliant does not make you compliant.

8. Your rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 you may ask us to:

  • Show you what we hold about you, and give you a copy.
  • Correct anything that is wrong.
  • Delete your data, where we are not required to keep it.
  • Stop or limit a particular use.
  • Object to anything we do on the basis of legitimate interest.
  • Hand it over in a portable format — and you do not need to ask us for your books, which you can export from Profitna yourself at any time.
  • Withdraw consent you have given, for advertising cookies or marketing email. Withdrawing it does not undo what was lawful beforehand.

Write to support@profitna.com. We will answer within 30 days, and we will not charge you for it. We may need to confirm who you are first, which is a protection for you rather than an obstacle.

9. How it is protected

  • Everything travels over HTTPS.
  • Passwords are stored as bcrypt hashes, never as text that can be read back.
  • Each organisation's data is separated, and every request is checked against the organisation it claims to belong to.
  • Any payment credentials a business stores are encrypted with AES-256-GCM, bound to that organisation so that a value copied between accounts simply fails to decrypt.
  • Administrative access needs a second factor.
  • Backups are taken on a schedule and verified by being read back, not merely written.

No system is perfect. If we discover a breach that is likely to harm you, we will tell you and the Nigeria Data Protection Commission, as the Act requires.

11. Email we send you

Three kinds, and we keep them apart deliberately:

  • About your account — your trial ending, a payment failing. Sent because you hold an account with us. These cannot be switched off; closing the account stops them.
  • Help getting started — short notes about the parts of Profitna you have not reached. One click stops them, and they stop on their own once you are up and running.
  • News and offers — marketing. Off unless you deliberately turn it on, and we record when and from where you did.

Every optional email carries a one-click unsubscribe link, and a link to a page where you can choose between the categories rather than only all-or-nothing.

12. Children

Profitna is for businesses and organisations and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe we have, tell us and we will delete it.

13. Changes to this page

When we change something that matters we will update the date at the top and, if the change affects you materially, tell you in the product or by email. We will not quietly broaden what we do with data you have already given us.

14. Complaints

Tell us first — support@profitna.com — because most things are a misunderstanding we can fix the same day.

If we do not put it right, you may complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission, the regulator established under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023. Complaining to them costs you nothing and does not affect your account with us.
